01Connect

Connect your e-wallet.

Thirty days of transactions are read through a licensed open finance provider, with read only access. From that history, Laju calculates a health score out of 100 across four pillars.

  • Read OnlyLaju can look. It cannot move money.
  • 30 DaysOne month of real transactions.
  • 4 PillarsCash flow, savings, debt, planning.

02Save

Give every rupiah a place.

The ladder tells you what comes first: spend within your daily budget, clear the debt that costs the most, then build a cushion. Every deposit is logged on one screen with a number pad.

  • Daily BudgetIncome minus instalments, divided by 30.
  • Costly Debt FirstOnline loans and paylater go first.
  • Mini CushionThree days of essential spending.

03Prove

Take your record to the bank.

Each finished step becomes part of a financial record a bank can assess. Laju is not a lender. It helps drivers become eligible for formal credit.

  • VerifiedBacked by e-wallet data, at most 30 days old.
  • Your ConsentNothing is shared without your permission.
  • Not A LoanLaju does not lend money.

The App

Simple steps. Real progress.

The app turns one big goal into six small steps, with one active at a time. Finish a step and the next one unlocks. Behind it is a score that follows the FinHealth Score framework from the Financial Health Network.

  1. 01
    Health Score

    Four pillars and twelve components make one number out of 100, so you always know where you stand.

  2. 02
    Milestone Ladder

    Tidy your data, get out of the red, clear short term debt, build an emergency fund, invest, then grow.

  3. 03
    Daily Budget

    Income minus long term instalments, divided by 30. One number to stay under each day.

  4. 04
    Debt Payoff

    Online loans, paylater, and informal lenders go first. A motorbike loan can keep running.

  5. 05
    AI Analysis

    A plain language summary of what is going well, what to fix, and what to do in the next 30 days.

  6. 06
    Two Languages

    Use the app in Indonesian or English.

Security

Prepare the umbrella before the rain.

  • Read only.

    E-wallet access goes through a licensed open finance provider. Laju can look, never move money.

  • You are in control.

    Revoke permission and delete all your data at any time.

  • No PIN, ever.

    Passwords are never stored, only a salted hash. Laju never asks for a PIN.

  • Statements are not kept.

    An uploaded PDF e-statement is read, then discarded.

  • AI sees numbers only.

    It never receives your name, phone number, or account details.

  • Never sold.

    Laju is not a lender and does not sell data.
