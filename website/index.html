The app

Plays like a game. Reads like a bank record.

Six steps, one active at a time. Each one you finish unlocks the next. Underneath is a score that follows the FinHealth Score framework from the Financial Health Network.

  1. 01
    Health score

    Four pillars, twelve components, one number out of 100. Cash flow, savings, debt and plan.

  2. 02
    The milestone ladder

    Tidy your data, get out of the red, clear short term debt, build an emergency fund, invest, then grow.

  3. 03
    Daily budget

    Income minus long term installments, divided by 30. One number to stay under each day.

  4. 04
    Debt payoff track

    Online loans, paylater and loan sharks go first. A motorbike loan does not have to be cleared to move on.

  5. 05
    AI analysis

    A plain language summary of what is going well, what to fix and what to do in the next 30 days.

  6. 06
    Two languages

    Use the app in Indonesian or English.

Safety, in plain words

Have an umbrella ready before it rains.

  • We can look. We can't touch.

    E-wallet access is read only and goes through a licensed open finance provider.

  • You hold the off switch.

    Revoke permission and delete all your data at any time.

  • No passwords kept.

    Only a scrambled hash is stored. Laju never asks for your PIN.

  • PDFs are read, then gone.

    An uploaded e-statement is read and discarded. The file itself is not kept.

  • The AI sees numbers only.

    Never your name, phone number or account details.

  • Your data is not for sale.

    Laju is not a lender and never sells data.
